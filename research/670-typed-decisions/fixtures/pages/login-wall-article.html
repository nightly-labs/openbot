How cities are redesigning streets for heat waves

By Maria Chen · 6 min read

Across southern Europe, city planners are replacing asphalt with permeable stone, planting shade trees along bus routes and repainting roofs white. The changes are cheap compared with the cost of heat-related hospital visits.
