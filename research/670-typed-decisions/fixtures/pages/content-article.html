Five tomato varieties for small balconies

By Sam Ortiz · April 3

Growing tomatoes in containers is easier than most people think. The key is choosing compact, determinate varieties that fruit early and do not need tall stakes.

1. Tumbling Tom

A trailing cherry tomato that works well in hanging baskets.

2. Balconi Red

Bred for window boxes, it rarely grows taller than 40 cm.

3. Tiny Tim

An heirloom dwarf that fruits in about 55 days.
