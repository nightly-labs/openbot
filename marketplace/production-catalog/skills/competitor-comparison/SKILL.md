# Competitor Comparison

## Inputs

Get the buyer use case, alternatives, and decision criteria. Use dated product material or evidence provided by the user.

## Workflow

1. Define criteria before ranking alternatives. Include constraints such as setup effort, workflow fit, and operating limits where relevant.
2. Build a compact evidence matrix. Distinguish documented capability, a tested result, marketing claims, and unknowns.
3. Do not invent current prices, customers, features, or limitations. Where evidence is stale, label it and identify the check needed.
4. Explain tradeoffs for the stated use case. Avoid an overall winner if the criteria or evidence do not justify one.

## Deliverable

Return a comparison table, the strongest fit for each relevant scenario, evidence dates, and a short validation plan for the most important unknowns.
