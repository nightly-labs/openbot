# Outreach Draft

## Inputs

Get the audience, offering, relationship context, channel, and verified reason for contact. If personalization evidence is absent, write an honest general draft.

## Workflow

1. Connect one relevant observation to one potential benefit. Do not fabricate familiarity, shared contacts, customer results, or urgency.
2. Use a clear opening, concrete value, and one low-friction request. Keep claims within supplied evidence.
3. Match the tone and length to the channel. Provide a small number of alternatives with a reason to choose each.
4. For a follow-up, add useful context or a different question rather than repeating the first message. Respect any stated contact restrictions.

## Deliverable

Return a subject line when appropriate, the draft, and a short note identifying claims to confirm. Do not send messages, collect contact lists, or start a campaign unless separately requested.
