### Fixed

- An automatic update no longer restarts OpenBot while an agent's question card waits for your answer. The update waits until you answer the card, for at most 24 hours. On this computer, the update settings show that it waits for an answer to an agent's question; a desktop or web client of a joined server and the phone app show it as other work. Before, the restart closed each open card as expired, and your answer could no longer reach the agent.
