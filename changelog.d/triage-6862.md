### Fixed

- When a message waited for the sign-in of the agent's provider and you selected a model of another provider, the change did not apply. The message waited with no end, and the chat asked you to sign in to the old provider. Now the new model applies at once and the message sends with it. If the new provider must also sign in, the chat asks you to sign in to that provider. On a joined server, the server must also be on this version.
