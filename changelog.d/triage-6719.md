### Fixed

- While agents work, the OpenBot window no longer slows down other apps that you use beside it. Before, the avatars, the working dots, the spinners, the task list and the light on the activity line of each working agent kept moving when another app had the focus. On macOS, the whole screen then drew again many times each second, so apps such as Safari felt slow. Now this motion stops when you use another app. It starts again when you come back to OpenBot or move the pointer over its window.
