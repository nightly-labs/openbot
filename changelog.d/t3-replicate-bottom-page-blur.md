### Changed

- Lists and pages that scroll now show a soft, gradual blur at the edge where more content is, in
  the desktop app and the web client. This includes chats, the sidebar, the agent dock, the
  library, tasks, settings, Marketplace, dialogs, menus and pickers. When you turn off blur
  effects, the plain fade stays everywhere.
- The public website shows a soft blur at the bottom of the window while you scroll a page.
