### Fixed

- In the desktop app, a video that an agent made on a joined server now starts to download only when you press Play. Before, each video in the chat downloaded in full when the chat opened, so the video that you wanted to watch waited behind the others.
- When a generated video cannot play, its card now shows a Retry button and keeps its Download action. Before, the card stayed on "The generated video is unavailable." until you opened the chat again.
- A video that you watch from a joined server now stays ready while you use it, so playing it again does not download it again.
