### Added

- Link your own WhatsApp account in Marketplace › Apps › WhatsApp account, as a linked device: scan
  a QR code on your phone, or type the code that OpenBot shows after you enter your number. Agents
  can then list your chats, read and search the messages on this computer, and download photos,
  voice messages, videos and files to Downloads/OpenBot WhatsApp. They can send messages and files
  (photos, voice messages, videos, round videos, documents) as you and mark chats as read only after
  you turn on sending. Unlink logs OpenBot out of WhatsApp. An owner or admin can also link the
  WhatsApp account of a joined server.
