### Fixed

- Text that you copy from OpenBot now pastes into mail, documents and chat apps without the styles
  of the app. This applies to Cmd+C or Ctrl+C in a chat, in the message box and on a formula, and to
  the Copy button of a message with attachments. Headings, lists, tables, links and code stay, and
  line numbers and button labels do not come with the text. Before, the paste had the colors of the
  app theme, such as white text on a dark background, and you had to paste without formatting.
