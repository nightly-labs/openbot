### Fixed

- In a channel, a message that you send while a member works on your request now goes into that work
  at once, in any language. Before, only a message that started with an English word such as "Also"
  or "Correction" did this. Other messages, for example a correction in Chinese, waited until the
  member completed the old request. This applies when "Steer agents while they work" is on and the
  member's provider can take messages during a turn. On a joined server, the server must also be on
  this version.
