### Added

- Help → Report a problem opens the bug report form on GitHub with your OpenBot version and system
  already filled in.
- Agents can report a problem in OpenBot for you. Ask your agent: it writes the summary and the
  steps, adds its provider and CLI version, and shows you the full report. When you approve it,
  OpenBot opens the issue on GitHub and your agent gives you its link. You do not need a GitHub
  account, and the issue does not show your name. When you are not signed in, you get the filled-in
  form to submit yourself.
