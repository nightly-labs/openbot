### Added

- Help → Report a problem opens the bug report form on GitHub with your OpenBot version and system
  already filled in.
- Agents can send a bug report or a feature idea for OpenBot for you. Tell your agent what went
  wrong or what you want: it writes a short text in plain words, without technical questions, and
  adds the app version and your system. You read it and select Send. OpenBot opens the issue on
  GitHub and your agent gives you its link. You do not need a GitHub account, and the issue does not show your name. When you are not
  signed in, you get the filled-in form to submit yourself.
