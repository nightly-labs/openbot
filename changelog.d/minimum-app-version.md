### Changed

- OpenBot now supports the releases of the last 2 months. When your desktop app or server is older,
  the app shows **Update required** in place of your workspace and installs the update by itself
  when your agents are idle; **Restart to update** installs it at once. A server installs it at its
  next safe point, after the running agent turns. An app that is offline or cannot reach the account
  service is never blocked.
