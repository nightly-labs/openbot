### Changed

- The chat rail now scrolls smoothly to a day that you select. Its fill, its reading head and the day count move together through the days between. Before, the chat jumped to the day and the rail snapped to it.
- The day count on the chat rail rolls to each new number and has the same neutral colour as the rail. Before, the rail had a lilac tint and the count was lilac.

### Fixed

- The reading head on the chat rail now shows the start of a day that you open near the end of a chat. Before, it showed the end of that day.
