### Changed

- A page that an agent shows in a chat no longer changes the window backdrop with one click. To use
  the page as the backdrop, click "More actions" (…) on the page, then "Use as backdrop". Before, a
  click on a small icon in the corner of the page (also an accidental click, or a click by an agent
  that uses the computer) put the page behind the whole window, and it stayed there after a restart.
