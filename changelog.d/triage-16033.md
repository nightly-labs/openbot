### Fixed

- Model picker: with Pool accounts on, each account that you leave out of the pool now has its own
  entry next to the pool entry, so you can give that account to one agent. The agent stays on that
  account until the account reaches a usage limit. Before, the account was not in the picker, and the
  pool moved an agent off it before its next message. On a joined server, the server must also be on
  the new version.
