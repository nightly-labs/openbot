### Fixed

- A channel task of an agent that runs one turn at a time now starts after the current turn of that agent. Before, when the agent got new messages in its own chat all the time, for example from teammates, each new message started first and the channel task could wait for a long time. Now the channel task starts before the messages that came after it. Messages that came before it still go first. On a joined server, the server must also be on this version.
- The lead of a channel now answers a person when two members already work in the channel. Before, the lead waited until a member task ended, which could take an hour. The lead now has a slot of its own. The members still share their two slots, and they cannot use the slot of the lead. On a joined server, the server must also be on this version.
- In a long channel, a channel task now starts in at most about 30 seconds when OpenBot makes the channel summary again. Before, each task in that channel waited until the full summary was done, which could take more than 30 minutes. The task now starts with the newest messages and tells the agent how to read the older ones. On a joined server, the server must also be on this version.
