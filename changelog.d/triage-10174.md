### Fixed

- When OpenBot's own copy of OpenCode does not start three times in a row, and you installed
  OpenCode yourself, OpenBot now starts your OpenCode. Before, OpenCode stayed off and refreshing
  the providers did not help. To try OpenBot's copy again, open Settings > Providers > OpenCode and
  click "Use OpenBot's version". For an agent on a joined server, the server must also be on the
  new version.
