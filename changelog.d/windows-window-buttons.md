### Changed

- On Windows, the app and chat windows have no system title bar. Minimize, maximize and close are
  at the top right of the window, in the style of the app, and the top row of the page goes up to
  them, as on macOS. Before, the system title bar took a row above the page. Drag the top row
  to move the window, and double-click it to maximize the window. To show the snap layouts, press
  Windows+Z.
