### Fixed

- The text that you type in the answer field of an agent's question card is no longer lost. If the
  card closes before you send the answer, for example because the agent stopped waiting or asked a
  new question, your text goes into the message box of that chat. If you open another chat and come
  back while the card is still open, your text is still in the card. Answers to private questions are
  not kept.
