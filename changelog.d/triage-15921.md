### Fixed

- In an allowed WhatsApp group, a reply to a message that an agent or a routine posted now starts the WhatsApp Orchestrator for 7 days after the post. Before, a reply to a post more than one day old was saved to the history with no answer and no reaction. A reply to a message that you typed yourself on your personal number still starts no work.
- In an allowed WhatsApp group, a mention of the linked number now starts the WhatsApp Orchestrator also when OpenBot did not know the WhatsApp id (LID) of the number. Before, such a mention was saved to the history with no answer and no reaction. On a joined server, the server must also be on this version.
