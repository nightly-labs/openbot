### Added

- Connect your own Telegram account in Marketplace › Apps › Telegram account. Sign in with a QR code
  that you scan in Telegram on your phone, or with your phone number, the code that Telegram sends
  and your two-step verification password. You need the api_id and api_hash of a Telegram app from
  my.telegram.org. Agents can then list your chats, read and search messages. They can send messages
  as you and mark chats as read only after you turn on sending. Disconnect signs OpenBot out of
  Telegram. An owner or admin can also connect the Telegram account of a joined server.
