### Added

- The website has a Pricing page. It shows the hosted servers at their Stripe prices, the Starter
  license (1 member) and the Team license (5 to 25 members), monthly or yearly, in EUR, USD or PLN.
  Choose a server, a license or both. The summary shows the total. Visitors in the EU see that the
  prices do not include VAT. A license bought with a server costs less (bundle discount), and yearly billing
  takes 10% off a license. When the Stripe prices do not load, the page shows the list prices.
- The header and the footer of the website have a Pricing link.
- The Pricing page has one button. It opens the web app at the add server dialog, or at Billing
  when you choose only a license.

### Changed

- A new Starter server has 1 member. Starter servers that already exist keep 3 members.
