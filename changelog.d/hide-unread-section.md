### Added

- Right-click an agent in the sidebar and clear "Show in Unread" to keep it out of the Unread group. Its unread chats stay in its own section and keep their badge. Use it for an agent that only gets work from another agent. The choice is saved on this computer, for each server. To remove the Unread group for all agents, choose "Sort chats" > "Manual" in the list menu.
