### Added

- Optional saved copy of joined servers: turn on **Settings → General → Keep a copy of joined
  servers** to see your agents, unread counts and the latest messages of recent chats at once when
  you open OpenBot, while a joined server connects. The copy is read-only, says that it can be out of
  date, and the server's data replaces it when the server connects. It is encrypted, stays on this
  computer without attachments, and is deleted when you sign out, change account, leave the server or
  turn the setting off. It is off by default.
