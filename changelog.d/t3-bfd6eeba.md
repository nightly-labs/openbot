### Added

- Your phone gets a notification when the work of a channel ends with an agent reply that you did not
  read, as the desktop does. The notification names the agent and the channel, and a tap opens the
  channel. The level, the mute, the preview and the switch of each agent apply as for a chat.
- Your phone gets a notification when a provider plan reaches its limit and your agents wait, as the
  desktop does. The reset time shows its time zone.

### Fixed

- The Dynamic Island and the phone Live Activity show the work of an agent in a channel or a side
  thread when it starts, and stop when it ends. Before, they could show the work late, or show it
  after it ended.
