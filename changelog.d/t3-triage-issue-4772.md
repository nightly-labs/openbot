### Changed

- On Windows, the app window no longer has a separate system title bar above the app's own top row. OpenBot now shows its own minimize, maximize and close buttons at the end of the top row, with the app's icons and theme colors. On Windows 11, the Snap Layouts menu no longer opens from the maximize button: use Windows+Z, or drag the window to an edge of the screen.
- On Windows, Settings no longer shows its own close button next to the window's close button. To close Settings, use the back button or Esc. Menus, lists and notifications open below the window buttons, not under them.
