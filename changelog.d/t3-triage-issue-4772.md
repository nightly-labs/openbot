### Changed

- On Windows, the app window no longer has a separate system title bar above the app's own top row. The minimize, maximize and close buttons now show at the end of the top row, in the colors of the app theme, as the window buttons do on macOS.
- On Windows, Settings no longer shows its own close button next to the window's close button. To close Settings, use the back button or Esc. Menus and lists open below the window buttons, not under them.
