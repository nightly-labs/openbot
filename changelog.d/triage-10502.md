### Fixed

- When an agent finds no saved login for a sign-in page, it can now tell you why and what to do.
  A saved login fits only the site of its website, and some services sign you in on a different
  site, for example Walmart Seller Center on `wal-mart.com`. The agent now asks you to add the
  address of the sign-in page to the login, and to keep the login where you share logins with
  OpenBot. Before, the agent only said that no login was saved. For an agent on a joined server,
  the server must also be on the new version.
