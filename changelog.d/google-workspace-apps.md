### Added

- Add Gmail, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, Google Slides and Google Calendar to the Marketplace. Sign in
  with your Google account and approve the access. You do not need to set up Google Cloud. Agents can read email,
  write drafts and change labels, find, create and copy files, edit documents, spreadsheets and presentations, and read
  and change calendar events. An agent sends an email only after you approve it on a card that shows the recipients,
  the subject and the body. An agent with full access sends at once when you asked it to send without asking.
