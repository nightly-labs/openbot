### Added

- You can upload an MP4 or MOV video to an Asset step on the Workflows canvas, or pick a video from the server library. The Asset step and the library show a frame of each video. Connect it to a Video step to use it as a clip. On a joined server, the server must also be on this version.

### Fixed

- The Download button on Image, Video and Asset steps on the Workflows canvas did nothing in the desktop app. It now opens a save dialog, also for a joined server.
