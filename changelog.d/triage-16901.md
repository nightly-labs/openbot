### Fixed

- When OpenBot's OpenCode 2 does not start, OpenBot no longer starts an OpenCode 1 that you installed in its place. OpenCode 1 cannot open the chats of OpenCode 2, so Settings told you to update OpenCode to version 2 while OpenBot already used version 2. Settings now shows the real start error of OpenBot's OpenCode. On a joined server, the server must also be on the new version.
