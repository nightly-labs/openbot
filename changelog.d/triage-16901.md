### Fixed

- When OpenBot's OpenCode 2 does not start, OpenBot no longer starts an OpenCode 1 that you installed in its place. OpenCode 1 cannot open the chats of OpenCode 2, so Settings told you to update OpenCode to version 2 while OpenBot already used version 2. Now Settings says that OpenCode did not start several times, and Diagnostics shows the start error of OpenBot's OpenCode. You can still choose your own OpenCode with "Use installed version". On a joined server, the server must also be on the new version.
