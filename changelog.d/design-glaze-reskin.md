### Changed

- New look based on the Glaze design system. The dark theme is pure black, the light theme is pure white, the accent is cobalt, and corners are rounder.
- Figtree is now the font of the app, the web client and the status page.
- Larger, easier-to-read text, and a centered chat column on wide windows.
- Main buttons, the send button and the focus ring are cobalt. The message box is rounder and stands up from the page.
- The sidebar is cleaner: bolder agent names, no role chip beside each name, and a taller search field. The open page in the side rail and in settings is marked in cobalt.
- Settings sections are white cards with a thin border (dark cards in the dark theme) instead of grey blocks.
- New icons throughout the app, from the Hugeicons set that Glaze uses, with a lighter line.
