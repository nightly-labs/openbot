### Added

- Cline is a provider. Its free models have a limit for each model, and the provider of a free model can use your prompts to train models. Select Download on the Cline row in More providers, then sign in with your Cline account in the browser, or set `CLINE_API_KEY`. OpenBot uses a `cline` 3.0.68 or newer that you installed yourself until a download exists. Cline agents stay on this computer: team members do not see them.
