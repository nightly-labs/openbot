### Fixed

- When an agent asks you to do a step on a browser page, such as a CAPTCHA, the full browser view
  now shows "I’m done" at the top, next to "Back to chat". Before, "I’m done" was only in the message
  box at the bottom of the page. When you hid that box, or it was hidden when the agent asked, the
  page had no "I’m done", and you had to go back to the chat to end the step.
