### Added

- A GitHub trigger of a workflow can watch all repositories of an organization or of your own
  account. In the repository menu of the trigger, choose "All repositories of <owner>" at the top of
  the owner's group. The trigger then starts on events of each repository of that owner that the
  OpenBot GitHub App reaches, also a repository that is made later, and archived repositories are
  skipped. To leave out one repository, turn it off in the menu: the other repositories of the owner
  then stay on by name. One trigger route holds up to 100 repositories. For a workflow on a joined
  server, the server must also be on the new version.
