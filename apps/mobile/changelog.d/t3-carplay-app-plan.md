### Added

- OpenBot is available in CarPlay. The car screen shows the pinned agents of the active server, then
  the agents with new messages and the other agents. Tap an agent to talk to it: it reads its unread
  replies aloud, then listens, as the voice conversation of a chat does. The avatar of the agent shows
  when it listens, thinks, speaks, or is muted. CarPlay needs iOS 26.4 or later.
