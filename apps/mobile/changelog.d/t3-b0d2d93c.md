### Added

- A new privacy setting, "Load remote content in chats", in Server Settings > Agents. It is off by default. While it is off, chats do not load pictures or site icons from other websites: links show with a plain link icon, a diagram that uses a picture from another website shows a "Load diagram" button, and pages from agents show without scripts until you tap "Run page". Agent backdrop pages show without scripts. Owners and admins change the setting, and it applies to everyone on the server. The server must be on the OpenBot version with this setting.

### Changed

- Links in chats no longer show the icon of their website until an owner or admin turns on "Load remote content in chats".
