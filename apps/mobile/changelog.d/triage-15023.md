### Fixed

- A one-finger drag in the agent's browser moves a slider, such as a "Drag to verify" check during
  a takeover. The browser now opens with Trackpad mode off, so a tap clicks where you tap and one
  finger drags. Before, Trackpad mode was on, one finger moved only the pointer, and a drag needed a
  double-tap and hold. You can still turn on Trackpad mode in the ··· menu.
