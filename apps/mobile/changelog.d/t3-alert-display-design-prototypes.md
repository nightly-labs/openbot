### Added

- After you use OpenBot on several days, home asks once for a rating. The lilac header reacts to
  each star. 4 or 5 stars open the App Store or Google Play. 3 stars open X with a post to the maker
  that you can edit. 1 or 2 stars open a public GitHub issue form, where you write what to fix.
  "Not now" asks again after 60 days, and an answer ends the asks.
