### Changed

- Pictures in chats and the Library load as small copies from the server, so a large Library opens
  much faster. In the image viewer, the HD button downloads the picture in full quality. Share, Save
  and Copy always use the full-quality picture. The server must also be on the newest version; with an
  older server, the app downloads the full pictures as before.
- Pictures, videos and files that the app showed stay on the phone between starts, up to 1 GB, so they
  open at once and without a connection. The Library also shows its last file list at once, while the
  server answers. Sign-out deletes them.
- The Library downloads only the pictures near the screen, not all pictures at once.
