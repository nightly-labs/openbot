### Added

- Hold the mic in a chat to dictate. The words go into the message box while you speak, and you can
  edit them before you send. Release to keep the text, slide up to keep dictating hands-free, or slide
  left to cancel. A tap on the mic still starts a voice conversation. When the message box has text,
  a mic next to Send adds more words. Dictation uses the recognizer you chose in Settings > Voice.

### Changed

- In an agent chat, Send and Stop in the message box have the agent's colour. Before, they were
  blue.
- In a channel, a tap on the mic dictates into the message box. Before, the words showed on a
  separate screen and you could only send them as they were.

### Fixed

- In a voice conversation, "Speak now" shows only when the microphone listens. Before, it showed while
  the microphone was still opening, and the first words were lost.
