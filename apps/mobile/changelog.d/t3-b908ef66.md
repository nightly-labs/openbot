### Changed

- Your analytics answer follows your account. When you change **Settings → Privacy → Share product analytics**, the account keeps your answer, and your other devices use it after sign-in. When you agree on desktop or web, the phone turns analytics on after sign-in. An older choice from a phone that was offline does not replace a newer one. If you turned analytics off before this version, an answer from another device does not turn it on again on that device. Only a change on that device turns it on.
